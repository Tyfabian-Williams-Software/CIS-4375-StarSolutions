Welcome to Joyeuse's Caribbean Creole Cuisine & Bar!

This is the Haitian spot — where Creole heat meets island soul. Come on in and savor Haiti’s bold, unforgettable flavors, one bite at a time! From our tender griot and crispy plantains to spicy pikliz, savory stews, and refreshing island drinks, every dish is a celebration of home-cooked comfort and Caribbean flair. Here, the rhythm of Haitian culture blends with the warmth of Florida’s sunshine — bringing family, flavor, and good vibes together at one table.
